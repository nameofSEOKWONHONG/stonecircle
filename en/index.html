Music and video playback

Play MP3, WAV, M4A and other music plus your videos right away. No separate codecs or VLC install needed.

  • Shuffle and repeat, playlists and photo slideshows
  • Full screen, and a mini player that keeps going on other pages
  • External and embedded subtitles, AI translation of SRT files

Screen recording and capture

Pick a monitor and record with system audio and your microphone. Recording starts after a 3-second countdown.

  • Record with a floating camera preview
  • Capture a full monitor, the front window or a region, copied to the clipboard
  • Shortcuts that work from any app, and you can change them

On-screen whiteboard

Draw right on top of your screen while you record or present.

  • Five pen colors and adjustable width
  • Eraser and undo (Ctrl+Z)
  • Turn it on or off anytime, with or without recording

Video compression

Choose a compression level and resolution to save a smaller file that is easy to share. The original is never changed.

Timeline editing

Place several videos on a timeline, then trim, split, arrange tracks and add fades before saving a new file.

  • Export the current frame as an image
  • Upload the edited video straight to YouTube

Sticky note windows

Every note opens in its own window with rich text, tables and images. Notes save automatically 3 seconds after you stop typing.

  • Pin on top, hide, snap to other notes and screen edges
  • Position, size and zoom are restored next time

Note reminders

Set a date, daily or weekly time and the note pops up above other windows to remind you.

AI chat grounded in your files

The AI searches and reads your notes and the local folders, OneDrive and Google Drive documents you register.

  • Text, Word, Excel, Google Docs and Sheets
  • Sources under every answer, note links open the original
  • Web search when needed, save any answer as a note

Workflows

Combine a question, documents, web search and specific web pages. At the scheduled time the AI gathers the material, writes it up and delivers a new note.

FAQ

Questions

Can I use them without AI?

Yes. MUZ playback, recording, capture, compression and editing, and YUMO notes, reminders, search, AI Paste, recording and transcription all work without an AI connection. AI chat, Workflows, AI meeting minutes and MUZ AI need one.

Which AI services can I connect?

OpenAI, Azure OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini and Ollama (local). You connect a key you obtained yourself or a local server you run.

Does AI Paste send what I paste to an AI?

No. Pasted content is checked on your PC against built-in rules and your own patterns only. AI is used only if you ask it to turn a pattern description into a regular expression, and then only the description and examples are sent.

Is my meeting audio uploaded?

No. Transcription runs on your PC with a Whisper model; the network is used only to download the model the first time. When you create AI minutes, only the transcript text is sent to the AI you chose.

Do MUZ and YUMO share data?

No. Each app has its own data folder, account connections and settings.

Will my original files be changed?

MUZ always saves compression and edits as new files, and removing an item from a list does not delete the original. YUMO only reads the documents you register.
